title: "Evidence and verification"
description: "Distinguish records, scoped verification, and authorization for an effect."
---

Rail evidence is append-only. A record preserves an assertion or decision; it is not a reusable grant of authority.

| Layer | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| Receipt and signature | Which bytes were committed and signed? |
| Inclusion proof and checkpoint | Is the record included in the asserted ledger history? |
| Verification disposition | What is asserted for a subject, scope, and verification dimension? |
| Projection | What does the applicable evidence establish for a fixed evaluation snapshot? |
| Effect decision | May one named operative effect proceed under current conditions? |

A complete projection does not automatically authorize result release, retries, downstream execution, or settlement. Those effects have separate gates.

## Causal links and correlations

Causal parents identify required dependencies under explicit roles and policy. Correlations help find related work. A correlation is not a causal proof, and timestamp order does not create a global causal ordering.

## What cryptography does not establish

A valid signature establishes integrity under a trusted key. It does not establish the truth of every external assertion, prove current authority, or qualify an integration for production.

Trust anchors must be established independently. Downloading a bundle and its accompanying key from the same untrusted location is not independent trust establishment.
