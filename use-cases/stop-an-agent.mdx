title: "Use case: Stop an agent everywhere, or end one journey"

Revoking grants one at a time is slow and permanent, and it loses the record of why. Contract 0.2.0 adds two stops with different reach: a **suspension** holds one principal everywhere (or in one project) and can be lifted, and a **termination** ends executable authority over one journey for good.

<Note>
  These operations are in customer contract 0.2.0. Confirm they are enabled in your deployment. See [environment and release status](/get-started/environment).
</Note>

## Suspend a principal

`POST /v1/principals/{principal_id}/suspensions` with `scope: all`, or `scope: project` and a `project_id`, and an optional `expires_at`. While the suspension is active:

- new executions where it is the acting principal are refused at the gate,
- capability tokens are not issued to it, and it cannot delegate or receive delegations,
- unclaimed dispatches it initiated are refused at the claim.

Each refusal is a signed denial with basis `PRINCIPAL_SUSPENDED`. Work that was already dispatched is not interrupted, and its results are still admitted. Grants are neither revoked nor consumed: a suspension is a hold, not a withdrawal. Every change of state is a receipt on the principal's stream.

## Lift a suspension under an approval

Creating a suspension opens a lift approval and returns its id as `lift_approval_id`. Approvers vote through the existing [approval flow](/guides/approvals); the suspended principal is excluded from the quorum. `POST /v1/suspensions/{suspension_id}/lifts` succeeds only with that approval approved. If the approval expires before anyone acts, a replacement opens and `lift_approval_id` reports the current one. A suspension with `expires_at` ends on its own, with its own receipt.

## Terminate one journey

A journey is the work that descends from one root grant. `POST /v1/journeys/{journey_id}/terminations` is absorbing: pre-dispatch executions are cancelled, in-flight effects are listed as unresolved, results already produced are still admitted, and restarting needs a new root grant. Enrolled evaluators can also submit signed safety signals (`POST /v1/journeys/{journey_id}/safety-signals`); the Rail's safety profile, not the evaluator, derives the disposition. Read the current disposition with `GET /v1/journeys/{journey_id}`.

<Note>
  Stopping an agent process and ending its authority are different operations. Your target adapters must still refuse effects that arrive without a current decision.
</Note>
