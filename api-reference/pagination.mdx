---
title: "Pagination"
description: "Traverse authorized snapshots with opaque cursors."
---

List operations accept `limit` and `cursor` where documented. `limit` defaults to 50, with a range of 1–200.

Keep filters unchanged across a traversal and pass the returned cursor back unchanged. Cursors bind to tenant, filters, and a snapshot; they are not offsets and must not be decoded or shared across tenants.

The contract specifies a minimum 15-minute snapshot retention window. An invalid or expired cursor returns `400`. Start a fresh traversal after expiry instead of guessing a replacement cursor.

The events feed has additional `after_event_id` semantics and a separate retention window. Do not combine `after_event_id` with `cursor`. See [events](/webhooks/events).

Pagination controls what you can read. It does not grant authority to act on a returned object.
