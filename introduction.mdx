The Rail is the **authorization and clearing layer for autonomous agents**. Govern what agents may do, verify what happened, and determine when value may be released.

Put Rail between an agent's proposed action and the system that performs it. Rail connects bounded authority, execution decisions, evidence, and downstream consequences. Your agent can keep running after its authority ends; the protected action must still be refused.

## How the pieces fit

1. **Authorize.** Bind the actor, scope, purpose, and applicable evidence to the proposed action.
2. **Execute.** Check current authority at the protected boundary, including changes since approval.
3. **Verify.** Preserve connected decisions and outcomes in inspectable evidence.
4. **Clear.** Determine whether value may be released under the applicable terms, rights, and evidence.

Clearing and execution are separate decisions. An action can execute while a later settlement remains held. A receipt documents a decision; it does not authorize the next action.
