---
title: "Reuse, lineage, and rights"
description: "Connect exact artifact participation to rights and allocation evidence."
---

A reuse workflow must identify the artifact version that actually participated, not merely the version selected in a plan.

## Record participation

Use contribution and outcome operations to capture the applicable evidence. Build a provenance graph with explicit roles and relationships. Correlation identifiers can help join a business workflow, but they do not become causal evidence by themselves.

## Evaluate rights

Rights records identify the applicable terms and constraints. A referenced rights record is not permission to ignore its scope, expiry, or revocation. Recheck applicable rights before a protected downstream effect or release.

## Compute allocation

[`computeAllocation`](/api-reference/endpoints/compute-allocation) evaluates the selected method and evidence. Participation can validly result in zero allocation. Positive shares, negative contribution records, residuals, and payable terms must not be conflated.

## Carry the evidence into clearing

Use the verified lineage, rights, and allocation inputs in the [realization and settlement flow](/guides/clearing-and-settlement). A rights dispute can hold release after the work has executed.
