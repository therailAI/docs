---
title: "Human approvals"
description: "Bind human review to the exact action and re-evaluate before execution."
---

Rail creates approval requests when policy requires them. Your application displays the proposed action and its governing context to eligible human approvers.

1. Read the approval using [`getApproval`](/api-reference/endpoints/get-approval).
2. Preserve the strong `ETag` response header.
3. Authenticate the human with the configured interactive flow and required assurance.
4. Submit [`voteOnApproval`](/api-reference/endpoints/vote-on-approval) with `Idempotency-Key` and `If-Match`.
5. Observe the execution after quorum. Fresh evaluation still applies.

```json
{"decision": "approve", "comment": "Reviewed the exact proposed action."}
```

The server determines approver eligibility, identity, and quorum. The voter cannot choose their own group or count multiple effective votes as different people. Client-credentials tokens cannot cast human votes.

If the vote fails with `412`, re-read the approval and review the current state before deciding whether to submit again. `428` means the required precondition was missing. Do not silently replay a stale approval with a new ETag.

Changing the action, policy version, or governing conditions can require fresh approval. Approval does not expand authority or override revocation.
